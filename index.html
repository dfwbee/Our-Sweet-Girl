A message from me

My Sweet Girl, my KB, my family. 💙 It’s actually crazy to think that we’ve been friends since 100 level because, honestly, I never expected you to become this important to me. I didn’t go into university thinking I would meet someone who would eventually become family to me. Somehow, somewhere along the wayyou went from being just another person I knew to becoming one of the biggest parts of my life. And when I think about our friendship, I think about all the madness. 😂 Like that time we went to your house during Ileya and somehow you, me and Ifunanya ended up going to that guy’s apartment. We were supposed to go home early the next morning, but obviously… we didn’t. 😭 And you were so pissed at me. I still laugh when I remember it because, somehow, even in our madness, those are the memories I treasure the most. I think we really became close before 300 level resumed. Something just shifted between us, and from then on, you became someone I knew I could always count on. You have supported me in ways I don’t think I’ve ever properly thanked you for. Remember when we went to Lagos to meet Dera? We barely had money, but somehow we still made that trip happen. I ended up spending the little money I had, and then the hairstylist went and absolutely destroyed your hair. 😭 We were broke, stressed and probably wondering why we even left home in the first place. And when we didn’t have money, you still found a way you borrowed money from Rashida. Looking back, those moments were chaotic, but they were ours. And my birthday hair… God. 😭 That hair would have been a complete disaster if you hadn’t given me your wig. You didn’t even make it a big deal. You just gave it to me because you knew I needed it. That’s the kind of person you are. You show up. And then there was one of the hardest periods of my life, the whole situation where I stole bundles from that guy’s house. I know how bad everything felt for me at the time. I know how easy it would have been for someone to look at me differently or judge me because of what happened. But you never did. Not once. You never made me feel like I was a terrible person. You never abandoned me. Instead, you kept telling me that we’d be okay. You kept encouraging me, talking to me, supporting me and reminding me that one bad situation didn’t mean my life was over. I don’t think you understand how much that meant to me. You have been one of my strongest sources of motivation, even when you probably didn’t realize you were doing it. You have been there for me when I was happy, when I was confused, when I was making questionable decisions 😂, when I needed advice, when I needed someone to console me and even when I didn’t know what I needed. You worry about me even when you absolutely shouldn’t. 😂 You check on me, you advise me, you comfort me, and somehow you always manage to make me feel like I’m not facing things alone. And at some point, you stopped being my friend. You became my family. You’re so much a part of my life now that I genuinely can’t imagine things without you in them. My mum asking about you doesn’t even surprise me anymore. My sister sees you as her own sister and loves you, and honestly, that’s because you’ve made yourself so easy to love. We’ve had so many adventures together. So many stupid moments. So many laughs. So many situations where we probably should have made better decisions. 😂 And somehow, through all of them, we’ve continued to grow closer. I don’t know what the future looks like. I don’t know where life will take either of us, or how much things will change as we grow older, but I know one thing: I want you in my life through all of it. Because you have become a part of me. And I don’t think I’d be okay if you weren’t okay. Your happiness genuinely matters to me. Your sadness affects me. When something is wrong with you, I feel it too. That’s how much you have become a part of my life. So on your 23rd birthday, I just want you to know that I am so grateful that 100 level me somehow found 100 level you. I’m grateful for every adventure, every laugh, every argument, every piece of advice, every hug, every “you’ll be okay,” every time you showed up for me and every moment that brought us closer. Thank you for being my friend. Thank you for being my sister. Thank you for being my family. I hope 23 brings you everything you’ve ever prayed for and more. I hope life is gentle with you. I hope you never forget how loved you are. And I hope that no matter how old we get, we can look back at all these crazy university memories and laugh about how we somehow survived them. Happy 23rd birthday, Íyún Mi💙 I love you more than I probably know how to put into words. And no matter where life takes us, you’ll always have me. Forever my Omobolanle. Forever my girl. Forever family. 💙
